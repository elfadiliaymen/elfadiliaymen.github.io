About

I'm a junior full-stack developer from Oued Zem, Morocco, trained in digital development at ISTA Oued Zem and ENAA. What drew me to engineering is the satisfaction of turning a real need into working software.

I approach problems by understanding the system first: clear data models, well-designed APIs, and simple, honest code. I'd rather build something small that works than something large that's fragile.
